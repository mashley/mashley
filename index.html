25 years helping founders turn ideas into traction.

Hi — I'm Michael Ashley, though most founders just call me Mash. I've spent more than 25 years at the intersection of innovation, strategy, and execution: co-founding and scaling startups (with four successful exits), guiding dozens of companies from concept to traction, and launching a startup incubator at San José State University, where I also taught hundreds of aspiring founders as an adjunct professor.

Today I pour that experience into Inspiring Founders, helping bootstrapped founders find focus, clarity, and alignment through peer groups, hands-on advising, and the smart use of AI. I also build Foclarity, a go-to-market platform for CEOs, and Radi8, an AI-powered marketing platform. The throughline in all of it: help you cut through complexity, focus on what matters most, and turn bold plans into real results.
